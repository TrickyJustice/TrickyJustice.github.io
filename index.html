01The person behind the papers

Driven by questions.
Drawn to building.

What does it take for a model to understand the world — and create something new within it?

My research spans generative world models, image and video generation and editing, 3D reconstruction, and disentangled representation learning. I’m especially interested in making generative models more controllable.

My graduate research in Computer Science at the University of Waterloo was advised by Prof. Sirisha Rambhatla and Prof. Charles Clarke.

Before that, I received my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from IIT Kanpur, where I was advised by Prof. Faiz Hamid and Prof. Laxmidhar Behera.
